5000 hp Dragster V8 + 10-speed for R 2016 (unofficial)
=======================================================

A race package for the Scania R (2016 / next generation): a 5000 hp V8 and a
close-ratio 10-speed with a torque converter, geared for standing-start runs
and for top speed. Nothing stock is overwritten - every factory engine and
gearbox is still selectable.

This is deliberately unrealistic. If you want a fast truck that still behaves
like a truck, use the haulage package instead; this one does not pretend.

ENGINE - "16.4 L V8 5000 Drag (unofficial)"
  5000 hp (3676 kW) at 3900 rpm, 10 000 Nm from 1200 to 3000 rpm.
  Revs to 5200. No factory Scania engine sets a rev limit at all, so they all
  inherit a low default and simply cannot rev - lifting that is what makes the
  whole package work.
  Never derates: no AdBlue consumption and no low-AdBlue power limit.

TRANSMISSION - "10-speed Drag AMT 5000 (unofficial)"
  10 close ratios, uniform 1.216 step, 4.20 down to 0.72.
  2.20 torque converter, 2.64 final drive, 0.10 s shifts.
  Top gear reaches 522 km/h at the limiter, so drag and not gearing sets the
  ceiling - roughly 400 km/h running bobtail.
  5-stage front-loaded retarder, because stopping is the actual problem.

BEFORE IT WILL DO ANY OF THIS
  Turn off the truck speed limiter: Options -> Gameplay -> Truck speed limiter.
  Otherwise you are capped at 90 km/h and none of the above happens.
  Consider also turning off police and traffic before a top-speed run.

NOTES
  Traction is the limit off the line, not power. A 6x4 chassis launches far
  better than the 6x2, which drives only one axle.
  The engine uses the factory V8 sound, which was never sampled near 5000 rpm.
  It holds up better than expected but it is not a race sound.
